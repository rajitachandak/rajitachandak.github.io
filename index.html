I am currently an Assistant Professor in the Department of Statistics at the University of Wisconsin-Madison. My research interests include nonparametric statistics, theoretical machine learning and econometrics.

Previously, I was a Bernoulli Instructor in the Institute of Mathematics at École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL). I obtained my PhD from the Department of Operations Research & Financial Engineering (ORFE) at Princeton University where I was advised by Matias Cattaneo. I also received my incidental Master of Arts in ORFE from Princeton. Prior to this, I completed my Bachelors of Science with Honors in Applied Mathematics and Economics at Brown University where I was supervised by Kavita Ramanan for my honors thesis.
